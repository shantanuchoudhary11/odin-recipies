Pesto Pizza

Description

A quick and flavorful pizza topped with basil pesto, melted mozzarella, fresh tomato, and Parmesan cheese.

  • 1 (10 ounce) can refrigerated pizza crust dough
  • 1/2 cup prepared basil pesto
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tomato, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons pine nuts
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).
  2. Unroll pizza crust dough onto a lightly greased pizza pan or baking sheet, pressing it out to the edges.
  3. Spread pesto evenly over the surface of the dough, leaving a small border around the edge for the crust.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the pesto, then arrange tomato slices on top and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.
  5. Drizzle olive oil over the top and scatter pine nuts evenly across the pizza.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for 12 to 15 minutes, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Remove from oven, let cool slightly, slice, and serve.
