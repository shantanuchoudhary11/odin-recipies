Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, cheese, and sauce. This recipe features a creamy chicken filling and rich tomato sauce for a comforting meal.

This easy lasagna features jarred spaghetti sauce for easy layering and rich tomato flavor.

  • 6 uncooked lasagna noodles
  • 3 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 1 (8 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
  • ¼ cup hot water
  • 1 cube chicken bouillon
  • 1 (26 ounce) jar spaghetti sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in the boiling water, stirring occasionally until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain.
  3. Place chicken in a saucepan and cover with water. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until chicken is no longer pink in the center and juices run clear, about 15 minutes. Remove chicken from water and shred with two forks.
  4. In a medium bowl, mix together 1 cup mozzarella cheese, cream cheese, hot water, and chicken bouillon until smooth. Spread 1/2 cup of spaghetti sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Layer with 3 noodles, half of the shredded chicken, half of the cream cheese mixture, and 1 cup of spaghetti sauce. Repeat layers once more, ending with noodles and remaining sauce. Sprinkle remaining mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Cover with aluminum foil and bake in preheated oven for 30 minutes. Remove foil and bake an additional 10 minutes, or until cheese is melted and bubbly.
